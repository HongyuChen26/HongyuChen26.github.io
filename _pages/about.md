I am a Ph.D. in Mathematics at the [School of Mathematics](https://math.hit.edu.cn/), Harbin Institute of Technology ([HIT](https://www.hit.edu.cn/)), Harbin, China. I am currently a visiting Ph.D. student at the [Division of Applied Mathematical Sciences](https://sfa.korea.ac.kr/eng/10287/subview.do), [Korea University](https://www.korea.ac.kr/) (Sejong Campus), South Korea. I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from [Northeast Forestry University](https://www.nefu.edu.cn/), Harbin, China.

My research interests lie in differential equations and dynamical systems, particularly bifurcation theory and spatiotemporal pattern formation in reaction-diffusion systems. I focus on the effects of diffusion, time delay, advection, domain geometry, and spatial symmetry on stability and bifurcation phenomena. I am also interested in the existence, uniqueness, and stability of positive coexistence steady states in competitive reaction-diffusion systems. To date, I have published five first-author SCI-indexed journal articles, with research appearing in journals such as *International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos*, *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - Series B*, and *Nonlinear Analysis: Modelling and Control*.

I welcome research collaborations and academic exchanges. If you are interested in my research, please feel free to contact me at [chenhongyu19982021@163.com](mailto:chenhongyu19982021@163.com).
